About Me

My initial interest in science came from my introduction to the field of genetics, and due to my curiosity regarding how entire organ systems were built from individual genes, I was eager to learn about how the human body developed. Consequently, as I learned more about the structures and functions of the human body, I grew an interest in understanding the etiology of chronic disease, the biochemical processes in which these conditions evolve, their impact on patient quality of life, and the therapeutic methods and medical care available . With this narrowed interest in the treatment of chronic conditions, I began volunteering in the RWJ Barnabas Hospital (NJ), combining my passion for direct service and the science I had previously learned. As I was able to help a patient understand his treatment and steps to manage his symptoms through serving as his Spanish Interpretator, I, therefore, began to understand the need for increased health accessibility.

I am now studying Biology B.S. UNC Chapel Hill, on the pre-medicine track. With the goal of improving health accessibility, I am an active volunteer with the Student Health Action Coalition , involved in bringing primary care resources to the underserved and uninsured population in Carrboro. Additionally, through my involvement with the UNC Chapter of Remote Area Medical Volunteer Corps , , I volunteer at mobile clinics in remote locations such as Henderson, NC and Luray, VA, providing free dental, vision, medical and women’s health services. Reflecting on my own health journey, I find that my purpose is to increase health literacy regarding chronic health concerns in order to aid individuals gain knowledge about their health care and consequently, the agency to make their own health decisions.

Future Plans

I plan to be involved in IBD research in the near future, focusing on the cellular and microbial causes in these conditions that lead to the state of autoimmunity to consequently produce therapeutic approaches that focus on the root cause of these conditions. I also aim to aid women and expecting mothers to be knowledgeable about their health care and have a stronger voice in the birthing process, through serving as a doula.
